# Interaction and motion review

| Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Transparent navigation over every section | Stable translucent Giada navy navigation | Preserves contrast while scrolling across light sections |
| Floating proof cards could reach the image edge | Cards inset consistently inside the hero visual | Avoids clipping and improves responsive composition |
| Clickable controls relied mostly on hover | Buttons, tabs, and accordion controls use a subtle pressed state | Adds immediate tactile feedback without excessive motion |
| Hover styles applied broadly | Hover effects gated to fine-pointer devices | Prevents sticky hover behavior on touch devices |
| Entrance motion always enabled | Reduced-motion mode removes nonessential movement | Respects accessibility preferences |

Motion uses short, direct durations and a single natural easing curve. No looping decorative animation, broad `transition: all`, or delayed product interaction is used.
